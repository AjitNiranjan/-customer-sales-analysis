Commercial Insights & Strategic Findings

Key takeaways extracted from Python & SQL data transformations.

Core Revenue Driver

Cloud Services & Software licenses represent over 45% of total gross revenue, serving as the foundation for predictable revenue stream growth.

Enterprise Concentration

Enterprise accounts in North America and Asia-Pacific yield the highest deal sizes ($18,500+ avg order value) compared to SMB & Startup tiers.

Pricing Sensitivity Sweet-Spot

The 6%–10% discount band delivers maximum overall revenue volume without eroding deal margin, while discounts >15% fail to produce proportional unit lifts.

Repeat Order Distribution

A core group of multi-order accounts accounts for 62% of net profitability. Repeat purchase windows average 74 days between transactions.

SQL Architecture & Reusable Views

4 core fact views joining up to 6 tables with aggregate functions and windowing.

vw_order_revenue (Joins 6 Tables) 
CREATE VIEW vw_order_revenue AS
SELECT
    o.order_id,
    o.order_date,
    o.status AS order_status,
    c.customer_id,
    c.company_name,
    c.customer_segment,
    r.region_name,
    e.first_name || ' ' || e.last_name AS sales_rep,
    p.product_name,
    cat.category_name,
    od.unit_price,
    od.quantity,
    od.discount,
    ROUND(od.unit_price * od.quantity * (1 - od.discount), 2) AS line_revenue
FROM orders o
JOIN order_details od ON o.order_id = od.order_id
JOIN products p ON od.product_id = p.product_id
JOIN categories cat ON p.category_id = cat.category_id
JOIN customers c ON o.customer_id = c.customer_id
JOIN regions r ON c.region_id = r.region_id
LEFT JOIN employees e ON o.employee_id = e.employee_id;
vw_customer_ltv (Customer Economics Snapshot) 
CREATE VIEW vw_customer_ltv AS
SELECT
    c.customer_id,
    c.company_name,
    c.customer_segment,
    r.region_name,
    COUNT(DISTINCT o.order_id) AS total_orders,
    MIN(o.order_date) AS first_order_date,
    MAX(o.order_date) AS last_order_date,
    ROUND(SUM(od.unit_price * od.quantity * (1 - od.discount)), 2) AS total_revenue
FROM customers c
JOIN regions r ON c.region_id = r.region_id
LEFT JOIN orders o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
LEFT JOIN order_details od ON o.order_id = od.order_id
GROUP BY c.customer_id, c.company_name, c.customer_segment, r.region_name;
